Norway is much much bigger than Switzerland. Much fewer people and traffic jam on the trails. By car you can just park and hike once in the high areas. Easy access by car on roads 7, 51, etc.

**Chunk 5:**
Probably the two best hiking countries in Europe. If you will just drive and tent, I think I just would have planned for both and waited until I could check the weather reports 2-3 days ahead. Bad weather often occurs in the mountains, both in Switzerland and Norway.

**Chunk 6:**
A million places to hike in both countries. If you just want to hike to a 1000m-ish mountain there are several options 1-2h from Oslo (like Norefjell, Blefjell, Gausta, Hafjell, Madonna Eggedal). I guess that will also be much less driving than to Switzerland. If you add a couple of more hours you could find some fjord+mountain combination (like Prest in Aurland / Geiranger and thousands of others). But be aware that there could still be snow at higher altitudes in the beginning of the Summer.
